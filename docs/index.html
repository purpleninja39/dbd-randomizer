This website, and the programmed challenge, is inspired by DBD creator streaks, where a streamer tries to win on all the killers at once but with specific rules!
I wanted to make my own challenge, but I'm definitely not good enough to do the same streaks as players like SuperAlf or Hens, so this challenge is pretty lenient.
This challenge in particular takes inspiration from the Ash to Iri challenge, where you need to reach a goal before you lose all your killers!
Here are the rules:
-The website will choose a random killer, one of their adept perks, and two random add-on rarities
-You choose three other perks to bring into the trial!
-If you lose on a killer, click the 'Record Loss' button. That killer, their perks, as well as any general perks you had equipped, will be disabled
-The goal? That's kind of up to you! Ash to Iri is probably the most challenging, but you could try and get 5,000,000 bloodpoints before you lose your killers!
-To reset the challenge, clear your browser's cookies!
